  • Logistic Regression baseline
  • Simple similarity/rules baseline
  • Majority-class baseline

LSTM must be compared with:

  • Naive last-value forecast
  • Seasonal naive (where applicable)
  • Moving average / exponential smoothing
  • ARIMA or statistical baseline

Promotion Decision

Promotion Gates

All gates must pass before promotion
● Reproducible dataset snapshot
● Successful automated tests
● No detected leakage
● Outperforms declared baseline
● Acceptable fold stability
● Acceptable holdout calibration
● Recorded fairness assessment
● Explainability output
● Complete model card
● Explicit authorised approval

Actions

Data Scientists may check gates, record test evidence, evaluate, or reject a candidate. Administrator approval is required to activate or roll back a model.
