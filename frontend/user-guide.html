What FUTURE does

The platform compares curriculum evidence with labour-market evidence to help staff investigate skill alignment, demand signals, and governed recommendations.

04

Read the evidence responsibly

  • Check provenance. Confirm the source, collection period, programme, and document version.
  • Check coverage. A missing skill may reflect incomplete evidence rather than a curriculum deficiency.
  • Treat forecasts as uncertain. Projections are planning evidence, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • Distinguish candidate from active models. Training does not make a model active; validation and authorised promotion are required.
  • Keep humans accountable. Recommendations require expert interpretation and governance review.

05

Common tasks

How do I review a recommendation?

Open Recommendation Review from the portal, inspect its supporting evidence and explanation, then record the permitted review action and rationale. Do not approve a recommendation only because it has a high score.

How do I add new data?

If your role permits it, use Data Operations. Select the correct source type, provide the required source information, run the quality checks, and resolve flagged issues before using the result.

How do I work with a model?

Use Model Lab to prepare labelled evidence and create a candidate. Review its dataset fingerprint, validation evidence, limitations, and comparison with the active model before any promotion decision.

Why can I not see an area?

Your assigned role may not permit access. Contact the platform administrator if your responsibilities have changed.
